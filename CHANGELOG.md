# Changelog

## Version 1.0.0 — October 2026

The very first release of Snugfit!

### Puzzles
- 10 puzzles to start: the 5-puzzle October Collection (Pumpkin Patch at Dusk, Haunted Hill House, Autumn Leaves, Halloween Candy Table, Trick-or-Treat Street) plus the Starter Collection (Boston Terrier Pals, Spring Flower Garden, Coral Reef Friends, Misty Forest Morning, Light Through the Glass).
- Four difficulty levels for every puzzle: Beginner (about 100 pieces), Easy (about 250), Medium (about 500) and Advanced (1,000).
- Every game gets freshly cut pieces with their own natural tab shapes.

### Playing
- Pieces snap together when they fit and lock into place on the board.
- Move connected groups together, pan around the table and zoom in and out.
- Helpful tools: picture preview, ghost image on the board, show edge pieces only, and Tidy, which gathers loose pieces with edges closest to the board.
- A celebration when you finish, plus your best time for each level.

### Saving
- Progress saves automatically, so you can stop any time.
- Continue your last puzzle from the main menu, or pick any of your saved puzzles.

### Options
- Separate music and sound-effect volume controls.
- Light and dark themes, with several table colors to choose from.
- Fullscreen mode (F11).
- Built-in updater that checks GitHub for new versions and installs them for you.

### Coming soon
- 5 new puzzles every month!
